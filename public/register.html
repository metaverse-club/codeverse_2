Important

Registration Guidelines & Instructions

Please read all the instructions carefully before proceeding to the registration form.

  • Team Composition: Each team must have exactly 4 members — 1 Team Leader + 3 Members. No more, no less. The Team Leader will be the primary contact for all communications.
  • Eligibility: VIT Bhopal students: Only hostellers are eligible to participate. Students from other colleges: All students are welcome regardless of location.
  • One Team Per Student: A student can only be part of one team. If your email or registration number is already registered with another team, you will not be able to register again.
  • Registration Fee: The registration fee is ₹400 per team (not per member). Payment must be made via UPI or Bank Transfer. Keep your UTR/Transaction ID ready after payment.
  • Payment Proof: You must upload a screenshot of the payment confirmation (JPG, PNG, WEBP, or PDF). Without a valid payment proof, your registration will not be processed.
  • Accurate Information: Provide accurate college email IDs, registration/roll numbers, and phone numbers for all members. Incorrect details may lead to disqualification. You can edit details later from the Student Portal until the deadline set by organizers.
  • Hackathon Rounds: Round 1 (Online): 15-20 November — idea evaluation. Round 2 (Grand Finale): 28–29 November at Audi 1 & 2, VIT Bhopal — 36-hour hackathon on campus.
  • No Refunds: Once the registration fee is paid, it is non-refundable. Please ensure all team members are confirmed before registering.
  • Code of Conduct: All participants must adhere to a professional code of conduct. Any form of plagiarism, misconduct, or rule violation will result in immediate disqualification.
