Important

Registration Guidelines & Instructions

Please read all the instructions carefully before proceeding to the registration form.

  • Team Composition: Each team can have 2 to 5 members — 1 Team Leader + 1 to 4 Members (Duo, Trio, Quartet, or Quintet). The Team Leader will be the primary contact for all communications.
  • Eligibility: VIT Bhopal students: Open to all students and all batches of VIT Bhopal (both day scholars and hostellers are eligible). Students from other colleges: All students welcome nationwide.
  • One Team Per Student: A student can only be part of one team. If your email or registration number is already registered with another team, you will not be able to register again.
  • Tiered Registration Fee: The registration fee depends on your team size:
    • DUO (2 Members): ₹250 / team
    • TRIO (3 Members): ₹330 / team
    • QUARTET (4 Members): ₹400 / team
    • QUINTET (5 Members): ₹470 / team
    Payment must be made via UPI or Bank Transfer. Keep your UTR/Transaction ID ready after payment.
  • Payment Proof: You must upload a screenshot of the payment confirmation (JPG, PNG, WEBP, or PDF). Without a valid payment proof, your registration will not be processed.
  • Accurate Information: Provide accurate college email IDs, registration/roll numbers, and phone numbers for all members. Incorrect details may lead to disqualification. You can edit details later from the Student Portal until the deadline set by organizers.
  • Hackathon Rounds:
    • Round 1 (Online): 15–20 November — Online Vibecoding & idea evaluation. Track and problem statement will be finalized after Round 1.
    • Round 2 (Offline): 28 November at Audi 1 & 2, VIT Bhopal — Idea + Prototype Dev Round.
    • Round 3 (Offline): 29 November at Audi 1 & 2, VIT Bhopal — Final Presentation Round before jury.
  • Prizes & Perks:
    • 25K Internship for winning teams, championship trophies, medals, physical certificates, and goodies.
    • Paid Internship opportunity for ALL teams that qualify for the offline round!
    • Goodies & food/meals provided for all participants!
  • No Refunds: Once the registration fee is paid, it is non-refundable. Please ensure all team members are confirmed before registering.
  • Code of Conduct: All participants must adhere to a professional code of conduct. Any form of plagiarism, misconduct, or rule violation will result in immediate disqualification.
