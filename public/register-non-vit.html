Non-VIT Participant Guidelines

Non-VIT Registration Guidelines & Instructions

Please read all instructions and policies carefully before proceeding to the registration form.

  • ⚡ Direct Entry to Round 2 (Offline Hackathon): External squads from all recognized colleges and universities are exempt from Round 1 (Online Vibecoding) and qualify directly for the 36-Hour Offline Flagship Hackathon on March 27–28, 2026 at VIT Bhopal University.
  • Team Composition: Each squad must consist of 2 to 5 members — 1 Team Leader + 1 to 4 Members (Duo, Trio, Quartet, or Quintet). The Team Leader will serve as the primary squad administrator.
  • Tiered Base Registration Pass: Fees are based on squad size:
    • DUO (2 Members): ₹250 / squad
    • TRIO (3 Members): ₹330 / squad
    • QUARTET (4 Members): ₹400 / squad
    • QUINTET (5 Members): ₹470 / squad
  • 🍽️ Optional Dinner Pass (₹200 / person): Day 1 dinner at the campus venue is completely optional. You can choose dinner passes for individual squad members or for all members inside the interactive cart.
  • Campus ID Verification: Every member must carry a valid College ID Card or bonafide student verification when reporting to VIT Bhopal University on March 27, 2026.
  • Payment & Verification: Pay securely using the dynamic UPI QR code or bank transfer. Keep your 12-digit UTR/Transaction Reference ID ready to submit alongside payment proof.
  • Non-Refundable Policy & Ethics: Registration fees are non-refundable once paid. Teams must follow the hackathon code of conduct and academic integrity.
