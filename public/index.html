The registration fee is tiered according to your team size:
• DUO (2 Members): ₹250 per team

• QUARTET (4 Members): ₹400 per team

Payment can be executed via UPI (scanning our verified QR code) or direct Indian Bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS).
