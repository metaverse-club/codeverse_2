Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about team rules, registration pass, UTR verification, and logistics.

The registration fee is tiered according to your team size:
• DUO (2 Members): ₹250 per team
• TRIO (3 Members): ₹330 per team
• QUARTET (4 Members): ₹400 per team
• QUINTET (5 Members): ₹470 per team
Payment can be executed via UPI (scanning our verified QR code) or direct Indian Bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS).

After completing the transfer in your UPI or banking app, copy your UTR / Transaction reference number and take a screenshot. Submit these on Step 5 of the registration wizard. Organizers verify and confirm your status directly through the portal and email. Registration closes on 20 November 2026.

CodeVerse 2.0 takes place on 28 & 29 November 2026 on campus at VIT Bhopal University.
• Theme: Open Innovation. No problem statement is provided by the organizers — your squad can choose and define your own problem statement!
• 28 Nov (Daytime) — Round 1: Deciding your problem statement & taking the live coding challenge. (Disclaimer: Shortlisting may happen after this round for VIT teams; all VIT teams may not be permitted for overnight).
• 28 Nov (Overnight) — Round 2: High-intensity overnight prototype development sprint with continuous mentoring, snacks, meals, and midnight coffee.
• 29 Nov — Grand Finale: Final evaluation, prototype demos & pitching before expert judges, followed by grand winner announcements.
• Direct Entry for Non-VIT Teams: Non-VIT external teams are NOT subject to Round 1 shortlisting and enjoy direct guaranteed access to the overnight round and finale!
