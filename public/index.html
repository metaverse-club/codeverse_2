Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about team rules, registration pass, UTR verification, and logistics.

Each team must have 4 members (1 Team Leader + 3 Members). No more, no fewer. Individual registrations or teams with a different count will not be accepted.

VIT Bhopal students: Only hostellers are allowed to participate. Day scholars of VIT Bhopal are not eligible.
Other colleges: Students from any recognized university or college across India are fully welcome. Cross-departmental and inter-college squads are permitted.

The registration fee is a flat ₹400 per team (covering all 4 members). Payment can be executed via UPI (scanning our verified QR code) or via direct Indian Bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS).

After completing the transfer in your UPI or banking app, copy your 12-digit UTR / Transaction reference number and take a screenshot. Submit these on Step 4 of the registration wizard. Organizers verify and confirm your status within 2–4 hours.
